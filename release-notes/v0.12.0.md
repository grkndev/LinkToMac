Both apps update: Android **0.11.0 → 0.12.0**, Mac **0.11.0 → 0.12.0**. Nothing about how they talk to each other changed, so either one keeps working next to the other's older version.

## Android: the app updates itself

Until now a new version meant opening the release page, downloading the APK and finding it to install. Now the app does the tedious part:

- It **checks for a new version in the background** (when the app starts, then every few hours — you don't have to open it) and **downloads the APK** once there is one, then checks that it really is the next version of this app.
- When it's ready you get an **"Update ready — tap to install"** notification. Tap it and Android's own installer opens; press **Update** there. The app never installs anything on its own.
- If the app is open, the update sheet shows the same thing as an **Install** button, with a progress bar while it downloads.
- **Settings ▸ System ▸ Download updates automatically** turns the background download off. (Checking for a newer version in About still works, and downloads when you ask.)
- After the update the link to your Mac **comes back by itself** — you don't have to open the app.
- The first time, Android asks you to allow "install unknown apps" for Link to Mac. It's one switch, and the app takes you to it.

## Android: a new look

Every screen is now built with Android's native Material 3 components instead of a mix of two styles:

- **Settings ▸ System ▸ Appearance:** light, dark or follow the system, and an accent colour (or your wallpaper's colours).
- **Smoother motion.** The status card changes colour when your Mac connects or drops, its battery/distance tiles open and close instead of popping, and the dot breathes while it's still connecting. The file transfer card slides in and its progress bar glides instead of stepping. Permission notices fold away once you grant them, and a new clipboard item opens into the list instead of shoving it.
- **Settings and Notifications:** the big title slides up into the top bar as you scroll, and content fades out underneath it instead of being cut off.
- **A wrong pairing code shakes the field**, and the Lock Mac tile confirms with a check once the command went out.
- The clipboard list no longer redraws every row when something new arrives.

## The Mac app is signed and notarized

Until now the Mac app was unsigned, so on any other Mac the first launch needed right-click → Open. It is now signed with a Developer ID certificate and notarized by Apple, so it **opens like any other app** — no Gatekeeper workaround.

## Finder ▸ Share ▸ "Send to Phone" works

Right-click a file in Finder, choose **Share ▸ Send to Phone**, and it goes to the phone's `Downloads/Link to Mac`. The menu entry has been in the app for a while, but macOS only runs a sharing extension from a properly signed app, so it did nothing before. Dragging onto the window or the menu-bar icon still works too.

## Smaller things

- **The Back button now sits next to the window buttons** when you open Settings, Clipboard history or About, with the screen's name beside it. **⌘[** goes back as well.

## Known limitations

- **Updating from 0.11.0 to 0.12.0 is a manual install** (the updater is part of 0.12.0 itself). From 0.12.0 on, updates come to the app.
- **Android always shows its own confirmation** before installing an update, and asks once for "install unknown apps". Both are Android's rules for apps installed outside the Play Store.
- **The background download uses mobile data too.** Turn off *Download updates automatically* if you'd rather it didn't.
- **After updating from 0.11.0, macOS may ask again** for Bluetooth (used by "lock when the phone leaves") and for notification permission: a permission is tied to how the app is signed, and the signature changed. Allow them once.
- **Dragging from Mail, Photos or Safari's download list** isn't supported (they hand over a promise of a file, not the file). You'll get a notification saying so; drop the file from Finder.
- Everything listed as a limitation in 0.11.0 still applies (1 GB per file, folders limited to 1,000 files, resume needs the receiver to still hold the partial file).

## Install

**Android:** sideload `linktomac-preview-0.12.0.apk`.
**macOS:** existing installs update themselves via Sparkle; new installs open `LinkToMac-0.12.0.dmg` and drag the app to Applications.
