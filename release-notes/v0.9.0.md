Both apps update this time: Android **0.8.5 → 0.9.0**, Mac **0.8.5 → 0.9.0**. Install both — file transfer needs each side.

## Send files, both ways

Until now the only thing that crossed between the two devices was the clipboard, and an image had to be squeezed under a 1 MiB frame to get there. Files are now split into chunks and acknowledged one by one, so the frame cap isn't the size limit any more.

### From the phone

Share anything, and the share sheet has two new entries:

- **Send to Clipboard** — for images. Downscaled to fit and dropped straight on the Mac's clipboard, ready to paste.
- **Send as a File** — for anything. Original quality, saved on the Mac.

Both also appear in the sheet's top row, next to Quick Share's devices, so it's one tap from a photo.

Share a PDF or a zip and only **Send as a File** shows up — the clipboard entry is for things you can actually paste. Neither one opens the app; a notification tracks the transfer and tells you how it went.

### From the Mac

- **Drop a file on the dashboard window** — the whole screen turns into the drop target.
- **Drop it on the menu-bar icon** — the panel opens with a drop zone, so this works with the window closed.

Files land in `Downloads/Link to Mac` on the phone (tap the notification to open one), and in the folder you choose under **Settings ▸ Files** on the Mac — `~/Downloads/Link to Mac` unless you change it.

### You can watch it go

Android shows a progress notification both when sending and when receiving. The Mac shows a percentage in the drop zone while sending, and a row on the dashboard while receiving.

"Sent" is a real delivery receipt, not a guess: the sender waits for the other device to acknowledge every single chunk before it says so.

## Also fixed

- **A full-resolution photo no longer risks taking the phone's background service down.** Resizing a modern camera image for the clipboard could exhaust memory; it's now decoded within a bound.
- **Fewer images rejected as too large.** Photos that used to fail with "can't fit under the frame budget" now compress far enough to go.
- **The Mac's name reaches the phone**, so the phone's share entries can say which Mac they mean.

## Known limitations

- **Finder ▸ Share ▸ "Send to Phone" does not work.** The extension ships inside the app and even shows up in the Share menu, but macOS refuses to run an app extension that isn't signed with an Apple Developer certificate — and this app is deliberately unsigned. Clicking it does nothing. Use drag-and-drop instead. The code stays in the bundle and will work the day the app has a signing identity.
- **64 MB per file.** Anything larger is refused with a message rather than failing halfway.
- Transfers are one file at a time; multi-select isn't supported yet.

## Install

**Android:** sideload `linktomac-preview-0.9.0.apk`.
**macOS:** existing installs update themselves via Sparkle; new installs open `LinkToMac-0.9.0.dmg` (right-click → Open, since the app is unsigned).
