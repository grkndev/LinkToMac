Both apps update: Android **0.12.1 → 0.13.0**, Mac **0.12.1 → 0.13.0**. What the phone and the Mac send each other only gained optional fields, so either one keeps working next to the other's older version. The Android app updates itself: you'll get the usual **"Update ready — tap to install"** notification. The Mac app updates itself too (**Settings ▸ General ▸ Check for Updates…** looks right away).

## Control what your phone plays, from the Mac

Turn on **Settings ▸ Mirroring ▸ Mirror media** on the phone (it is off by default) and the Mac shows what the phone is playing and controls it:

- **Now Playing** is a new page in the Mac's sidebar: the cover, title, artist and album, a seek bar you can click or drag, previous / play-pause / next, and the phone's volume. When nothing plays, it says so.
- The **Overview** has a smaller player (click it to open the page), and the **menu-bar panel** shows one too, with a seek bar and the volume.
- **Settings ▸ Media** on the Mac can hide all of it, and can show **the song title next to the menu-bar icon** ("Midnight Ferry · Lumen Coast", with a pause sign while paused). That one is off by default.
- On the phone it needs only the **Notification access** the app already asks for: no Wireless Debugging and no daemon, so it works on Mobile Hotspot too. It sends the state when it changes and never writes a title into the logs.

## Mac: send several files and folders at once

- **Finder ▸ Share ▸ Send to Phone** used to appear only when exactly one item was selected. It is now offered for up to 500, and everything goes as one batch with one summary.
- **Dragging from Mail, Photos and Safari's download list** can be sent. These apps hand over a promise to write the file rather than a file, which used to end in "Couldn't read N items". *This one was built from the documentation and has not yet been seen working on a real drag.*

## Fixes and a safer hand-off

- **A resumed transfer could glue a different file onto an old partial one.** A different file with the same name and size could be mistaken for the half-sent one. The check now includes the file's real modification time.
- **The Finder "Send to Phone" hand-off no longer puts a file path in a URL.** `linktomac://send?path=…` could be opened by any app or web page and make the Mac send any readable file to your phone. The link now carries only a one-time id (it expires after two minutes), and opening the old form by hand does nothing.

## Send diagnostics

When something doesn't work, **Settings ▸ Support ▸ Send diagnostics** builds a report **on your phone** and shows you all of it before anything leaves:

- the app version, your phone model and Android version,
- the state of the link to your Mac and of each permission,
- **why Android last ended the app** (low memory, closed by you, crash, not responding, …), which is the usual cause of "it stopped syncing by itself",
- the last lines of the app's and the daemon's logs, with your data removed.

**Share report** opens Android's share sheet, so you pick where it goes. **GitHub issue** opens a pre-filled issue form with a short summary; attach the full report as a file after you've read it. **GitHub issues are public.** Nothing is sent automatically, there is no telemetry, and the report is deliberately not copied to the clipboard (the app's own clipboard sync would forward it to your Mac).

## Your data stays out of the logs

The old **Logs ▸ Share** passed log lines on as they were, and those lines could include the start of what you had copied, file names, your relay's address and your Mac's IP. That is fixed in two layers:

- The app and the daemon now **log lengths, counts and states**, not clipboard text, file names, addresses or package names.
- **Logs, Daemon Log and the report** also remove them again before showing or sharing anything: clipboard text, file names, addresses and IPs, your Mac's and phone's names, package names, content paths and the pairing room and key. Debug builds still show the raw log on screen so a developer can read their own; **Share is always cleaned.**

This cleaning is best-effort: it recognises addresses, paths, package names and your own device names, but cannot recognise an arbitrary word. That's why you read the report before you share it.

## Known limitations

- **Media: some of the design isn't there yet.** The phone doesn't send shuffle/repeat, the list of other players, or the speaker or headphones it is playing on, so the Mac doesn't show them. Media keys and Control Center on the Mac don't control the phone yet.
- **Media needs Notification access.** Without it the Mac says "Allow notification access on your phone".
- **"Recent exits" needs this version.** Android only keeps that record for the app that asks, so the section starts filling in after you update; an older APK shows "Not available in this build".
- **The daemon log is read the way it always was.** If the daemon is down and Wireless debugging is off, the report says the daemon log could not be read instead of waiting forever (it gives up after 15 s).
- **Updating is the usual update.** The app downloads this APK in the background and you confirm the install in Android's own installer; your pairing and settings are kept. It is a new version rather than a quiet update because native code changed.
