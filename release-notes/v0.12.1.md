Android updates **0.12.0 → 0.12.1**. The Mac app is unchanged (still **0.12.0**), and the two keep working next to each other as before. The app updates itself: you'll get the usual **"Update ready — tap to install"** notification.

## A new icon

The app icon is redrawn: a laptop with two-way sync arrows cut into its screen, on a deep blue. It replaces the old phone-and-laptop picture, which carried the Apple and Android logos. It works in every launcher shape (circle, squircle, rounded square), and with **themed icons** on Android 13+ it follows your wallpaper colours. The notification icon and the laptop icon on Home are the same mark.

- On Home, the laptop badge is a plain outline while your Mac is disconnected and fills in two tones when it connects.

## An animated start-up

Opening the app now plays a short intro instead of a still logo:

- On Android 12 and newer, the laptop pops in, its base stretches out, the two arrows slide in from opposite sides, and **"Link to Mac"** slides out from underneath it. It follows light and dark mode, and the screen keeps the intro up until it has finished playing.
- When the app is still connecting, the arrows keep sliding and a status line with a small progress bar appears below the name.
- Once you're in, the icon shrinks into the badge at the top of Home while the screen fades to your theme.
- On Android 11 the icon is shown without the animation.

## Known limitations

- **The intro can add a short wait.** To let it finish, the start-up screen stays up for a moment after the app is ready (at most about 0.7 s). The animation itself is Android's, so a few phones may draw it slightly differently.
- **Updating from 0.12.0 is the usual update.** The app downloads this APK in the background and you confirm the install in Android's own installer; your pairing and settings are kept. The new icon and start-up look arrive with the install itself, which is why this is a new version rather than a quiet update.
