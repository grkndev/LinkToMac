Both apps update: Android **0.12.0 → 0.12.1**, Mac **0.12.0 → 0.12.1**. Nothing about how they talk to each other changed, so either one keeps working next to the other's older version. The Android app updates itself: you'll get the usual **"Update ready — tap to install"** notification. The Mac app updates itself too (**Settings ▸ General ▸ Check for Updates…** looks right away), or you can install the DMG below.

## Android: a new icon

The app icon is redrawn: a laptop with two-way sync arrows cut into its screen, on a deep blue. It replaces the old phone-and-laptop picture, which carried the Apple and Android logos. It works in every launcher shape (circle, squircle, rounded square), and with **themed icons** on Android 13+ it follows your wallpaper colours. The notification icon and the laptop icon on Home are the same mark.

- On Home, the laptop badge is a plain outline while your Mac is disconnected and fills in two tones when it connects.

## Android: an animated start-up

Opening the app now plays a short intro instead of a still logo:

- On Android 12 and newer, the laptop pops in, its base stretches out, the two arrows slide in from opposite sides, and **"Link to Mac"** slides out from underneath it. It follows light and dark mode, and the screen keeps the intro up until it has finished playing.
- When the app is still connecting, the arrows keep sliding and a status line with a small progress bar appears below the name.
- Once you're in, the icon shrinks into the badge at the top of Home while the screen fades to your theme.
- On Android 11 the icon is shown without the animation.

## Mac: redesigned

The Mac app is rebuilt around a **sidebar** and follows **light and dark** mode. The window can be resized (down to 900 × 600) and remembers its size.

- **Overview** is the new home: your phone's name and state, the link (LAN or relay), its battery and distance, quick actions, anything arriving right now, and your latest notifications and copies.
- **Messages** is a conversation list with search next to the open conversation. A message that didn't go out shows **Not delivered · Retry**. You can reply to one-to-one threads from the Mac (not to groups).
- **Files** is new: a history of what you sent and received, the transfer in progress with its progress bar and Cancel, and a hint to drop files anywhere. Click a received file to open it; click a failed one to resume it. A folder that only partly arrived can't be resumed, because sending it again would deliver the finished files twice.
- **Clipboard** and **Notifications** get the same list design; lists scroll instead of paging by 7. Calls, Photos, media, Lock Phone and Cast Screen are shown dimmed as "Soon".
- **Settings** has its own window (**⌘,** or the gear) with five tabs: General, Clipboard, Notifications, Security, Connection. The relay form is now the Connection tab, with Save and Revert.
- **The menu-bar panel** is redrawn: status first, **Open Link to Mac**, then Settings… and Quit. Dragging files onto the icon still opens it with a drop zone.
- **Pairing starts the app.** A new install (and "Unpair") now opens **Pair your phone** with the QR and three steps, and closes itself when the phone connects. It replaces the old hidden sheet, which was in Turkish and described a screen the phone no longer has. **Settings ▸ Security ▸ Show QR Code…** brings it back.
- **A new icon**: the same laptop-and-arrows mark as the Android app, for the Dock (with dark and tinted looks) and the menu bar.
- **A designed install window**: opening the DMG shows an arrow from the app to Applications instead of a bare Finder window.
- Mirrored notifications now say which phone they came from ("from Galaxy S24 Ultra").

## Mac: smaller fixes

- **The menu-bar panel opens on the first click.** It sometimes needed several clicks, brought the app window forward with it, and made the menu bar flicker when the window was open.
- **Open Link to Mac** from the panel (and the gear) now come to the front instead of opening behind the app you were in.
- The window buttons sit inside the sidebar instead of on its edge, and launching the app no longer takes focus from what you were doing.

## Known limitations

- **The intro can add a short wait.** To let it finish, the start-up screen stays up for a moment after the app is ready (at most about 0.7 s). The animation itself is Android's, so a few phones may draw it slightly differently.
- **Updating from 0.12.0 is the usual update.** The app downloads this APK in the background and you confirm the install in Android's own installer; your pairing and settings are kept. The new icon and start-up look arrive with the install itself, which is why this is a new version rather than a quiet update.
- **Mac: some things are placeholders.** Calls, Photos, the media card, Lock Phone and Cast Screen are dimmed "Soon" entries with nothing behind them yet.
- **Mac: history lives in memory.** Messages, notifications, copies and the file history are cleared when you quit the app or unpair.
- **Mac: the icon's name in the install window reads "LinkToMac"**, the file name; and if Finder's path bar and status bar are on, the line at the bottom of the window is clipped.
